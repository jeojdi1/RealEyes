        01 · The system
        
Five instruments. One eye.

        
RealEyes is not a single sensor. It is a coordinated stack of five micro-instruments laminated into a single hydrogel disc thinner than a human hair. Scroll to disassemble.

      


      

        


          

            


              

                LR_01
                
                

                  01 / 05
                  
Iris Scanner

                  
192-zone biometric mesh. Pairs the lens to the subject's optic signature in under 400ms.

                

              


              

                LR_02
                
                

                  02 / 05
                  
Truth Teller Core

                  
Micro-expression and pupil-dilation analysis at 240Hz. Reads what muscle tone cannot suppress.

                

              


              

                LR_03
                
                

                  03 / 05
                  
Neural Link

                  
Encrypted bridge to the wearer's neural cortex. Interprets intent before language assembles it.

                

              


              

                LR_04
                
                

                  04 / 05
                  
Loyalty Engine

                  
Continuous fidelity scoring from 0 to 100. Calibrated against thirty-one behavioural axes.

                

              


              

                LR_05
                
                

                  05 / 05
                  
Threat Predictor

                  
Pre-betrayal alert. Surfaces deviation up to seventy-two hours before action is taken.
